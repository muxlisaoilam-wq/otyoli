Ot Yo'li – Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how users of the game Ot Yo'li (Knight's Path) can request deletion of their account and the data linked to it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to olmasbeknabiyev2@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Ot Yo'li - Delete my account.
  3. Write the Google account email address you used to sign in to the game, so we can find your account.
  4. We will confirm your request and delete your data.
Your request will be processed within 30 days.

What data is deleted

  • Your email address
  • Your user ID created by sign-in
  • Your saved game progress (completed levels and results)

After deletion this data is permanently removed from our servers (Firebase). Nothing is kept afterwards.

Playing without an account

You can play as a guest ("Mehmon") without signing in. In guest mode, no account is created and no data is stored on our servers, so there is nothing to delete.

Ot Yo'li – Akkaunt va ma'lumotlarni o'chirish

Bu sahifada Ot Yo'li o'yini foydalanuvchilari o'z akkaunti va unga bog'liq ma'lumotlarni qanday o'chirishi tushuntirilgan.

O'chirishni qanday so'rash mumkin

  1. olmasbeknabiyev2@gmail.com manziliga xat yuboring.
  2. Mavzu: Ot Yo'li - Delete my account.
  3. Xatda o'yinga kirishda ishlatgan Google akkaunt emailingizni yozing.
  4. So'rovingizni tasdiqlab, ma'lumotlaringizni o'chiramiz.
So'rov 30 kun ichida bajariladi.

Qaysi ma'lumotlar o'chiriladi

  • Email manzilingiz
  • Kirish paytida yaratilgan foydalanuvchi ID'si
  • Saqlangan o'yin progressingiz (o'tilgan darajalar va natijalar)

O'chirilgandan keyin ma'lumotlar serverlarimizdan (Firebase) butunlay olib tashlanadi va hech narsa saqlanib qolmaydi.

Akkauntsiz o'ynash

Kirmasdan «Mehmon» sifatida o'ynash mumkin. Bu rejimda akkaunt yaratilmaydi va serverlarimizda ma'lumot saqlanmaydi, shuning uchun o'chiriladigan narsa yo'q.
